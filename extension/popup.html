How to use
  1. Drag across the job description you want to use.
  2. Click the Flash Resume button on the right edge.
  3. Only the selected job description is sent to the local engine — no full-page parsing.
  4. Use this popup to choose between Truthful and Interview mode.
  5. The 1-page PDF is saved to Desktop/FlashResume and dropped straight into the form's upload box.
